Albert Einstein

About

Albert Einstein was born in March 14, 1879 to Hermann and Pauline Einstein. He is best known for his contributions to the field of theoretical physics, including his groundbreaking theory of relativity as well as the mass-energy equivalence formula. It's no wonder he received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921.

Dr. Einstein also made contributions to the realm of politics. He helped found the German Democratic Party, and leaned ever closer to socialism as time went by. He became a refugee of the United States during World War II, calling for America to enter the nuclear arms race since Germany was working on atomic bombs. This call would be a great regret near the end of his life.

Dr. Einstein passed away on April 18, 1955.
